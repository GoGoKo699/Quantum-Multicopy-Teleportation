# Scientific route

An unknown operation can be stored in Choi states before its input arrives. Ordinary port-based retrieval spends many stored copies to avoid a correction depending on that unknown operation. Repeated copies of the eventual input provide a second resource. This project determines how that second resource changes the fixed-error budget in the standard port-selection model.

The exact statement and all qualifications live in [model and frontier](model.md). The deterministic approximate theorem has three proof components.

1. [A physical collective instrument](construction.md) chooses an input specimen and a Bell port together. Every routed branch has the same polar output on the supplied symmetric-copy family. The first two moments of its Bell-sum operator give a fidelity bound including returned specimens.
2. [A port-selection converse](converse.md) averages the exact score over pure inputs. The resulting positive operator bounds every sender measurement and matches the constructive resource order.
3. [An Alice-only implementation](implementation.md) coherently selects the local Bell contraction and approximates its polar part. A cutoff justified by the spectral weight of the supplied inputs avoids an unpromised minimum singular value. Known gates, workspace, finite precision and routing are charged.

The [exact-return theorem](exact-return.md) gives the sharp success probability for exact output and exact Bell-pair return, the success–fidelity frontier, and the number of Bell pairs that must be spent for constant exact-output success.

[Examples and interpretation](examples.md) explain why the intermediate unequal-budget regime is consequential, why the balanced point alone is not an advantage over input estimation, and how the channel-retrieval application differs from arbitrary channel simulation. [Related work](../references/RELATED_WORK.md) credits the inherited machinery and fixes the comparison boundaries. [Verification](../verification/README.md) records the finite checks and what they do not establish.

The results cover deterministic approximation and heralded exact return under correction-free port selection. Stored-channel retrieval inherits its construction; its converse is not asserted for unrestricted global programmable processors.
